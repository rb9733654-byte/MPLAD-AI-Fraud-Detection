What it supports

One view of the signals that matter.

A practical foundation for programme teams to prioritise review—not a replacement for human investigation or audit.

01

Portfolio monitoring

Track project status, allocations, expenditure, and delivery across locations and categories.

02

Risk prioritisation

Surface projects with sample risk levels so review effort can be directed where it is most needed.

03

Anomaly review

Bring expenditure variance, delay, and pattern alerts together in a single review queue.

How the platform will work

From records to a more informed review.

  1. 01

    Connect programme data

    Project records, fund allocation, expenditure and progress data will be securely supplied through the planned REST API.

  2. 02

    Assess meaningful signals

    A future AI service can evaluate data patterns and return explainable risk indicators for reviewer attention.

  3. 03

    Review and act

    Monitoring teams review flagged records, inspect the supporting information, and follow established governance processes.
